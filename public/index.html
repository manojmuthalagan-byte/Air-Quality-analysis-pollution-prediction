01 THE INDEX

How an AQI estimate is formed

Each available concentration is mapped to its published breakpoint range. The sub-index uses linear interpolation, and the largest available pollutant sub-index is shown as the overall estimate.

I = (Ihi − Ilo) / (BPhi − BPlo) × (C − BPlo) + Ilo

The 2014 source publishes open-ended Severe concentration bands. This app reports 401+ as a lower bound there because no finite upper concentration breakpoint is published in that table.

03 WHAT TO KEEP IN MIND

Limits are part of the result

  • Sensor gaps and inconsistent sampling can distort trends.
  • Fire, festival, traffic, and weather events can change rapidly.
  • Hourly uploads are averaged by calendar day here; verify official averaging windows (24 hours for most pollutants, 8 hours for CO and O₃).
  • Wind direction uses circular means and sine/cosine correlation transforms, not linear degrees.
  • Duplicate checks find exact timestamps only; same-day repeats and station identity need dataset-specific review.
  • A model trained in one place may not transfer to another.
  • The demo series is synthetic and has no real city or station provenance.
